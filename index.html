Parents' Questions About Millennium Kidss

Find quick answers about our preschool, daycare, programs and locations in Nagpur.

What is Millennium Kidss?

Millennium Kidss is a preschool and daycare in Nagpur providing playgroup, nursery, kindergarten and daycare programs in a safe, caring and activity-based learning environment.

Which preschool programs are available?

Millennium Kidss offers Playgroup, Nursery, KG-1 and KG-2 programs designed for age-appropriate early learning and overall child development.

Where is Millennium Kidss located in Nagpur?

Millennium Kidss serves families across Khamla, Narendra Nagar, Nandanvan, Medical and Manewada in Nagpur.

Does Millennium Kidss offer daycare?

Yes. Millennium Kidss offers daycare services for children with full-day care, play, learning activities and after-school support.

What makes Millennium Kidss different?

Millennium Kidss combines early learning, play, communication, creative activities and caring support to help children learn and grow in a joyful environment.
