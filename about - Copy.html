About Us

The Best Preschool, Daycare and Activity center

Aayu Educare Society proudly runs Millennium Kidss Preschool & Daycare in Nagpur, established in 2016. We follow an updated curriculum aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2022) for pre-primary education, ensuring developmentally appropriate and future-ready learning for young children. At Millennium Kidss, we offer a play-based learning program for children aged 1 to 6 years, focusing on cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development. Our centers are conveniently located in Khamla, Narendra Nagar, Medical, Nandanvan, and Manewada, providing both Pre-Primary School and Daycare services. Our philosophy is to create a safe, nurturing, and child-friendly environment where learning is holistic, joyful, and stress-free. We believe that learning is a lifelong process, and early childhood education lays the foundation for confident, happy, and curious learners.

  • Comprehensive reporting on individual achievement
  • Educational field trips and school presentations
  • Individual attention in a small-class setting
  • Learning program with after-school care
